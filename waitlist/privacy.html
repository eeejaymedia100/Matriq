Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Matriq collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Matriq mobile app, website and API (together, the Service).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, faculty, department, level, and (for staylite students) matriculation number or JAMB registration number.
  • Verification documents: photos of your student ID card or university portal screenshots, uploaded during identity verification.
  • Payment records: the amount, date and status of dues payments, stored by Matriq and the association you pay.
  • Usage data: basic activity such as announcements read, event RSVPs and AI queries, used to provide and improve the Service.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and association membership.
  • To verify your student identity, in cooperation with association executives.
  • To process and record dues payments and issue receipts.
  • To deliver announcements, events, and AI study answers you request.
  • To keep the Service secure and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Who sees your data

Verification documents are visible only to the executives of the association you are joining. Your name and level may be visible to other members of the same association, as needed for association operations. We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Third-party services

Payments are processed by third-party providers such as Paystack under their own privacy policies. Push notifications are delivered through the ntfy service. We use these providers only to operate the Service, and we limit what we share to what each provider needs.

5. AI Study Companion

Questions you ask the AI Study Companion are sent to our AI service to generate an answer grounded in your association's approved study materials. Your conversations are used to provide the answer and improve the companion; do not include sensitive personal information in your questions.

6. Data retention & security

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active, and verification documents for as long as required for the association's records. Data is stored with encryption in transit and at rest, on servers located in the European Union (Google Cloud Platform). We restrict access to your data to authorised personnel and executives only.

7. Your rights

You can update your profile details in the app at any time. You may request deletion of your account and data by contacting the support channel provided in the app; we will action eligible requests within 30 days, subject to legal or association record-keeping requirements.

8. Children's privacy

Matriq is intended for tertiary institution students who are 16 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app and by updating the version and effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us via the support channel provided in the app, or the contact details on the Matriq waitlist page.
