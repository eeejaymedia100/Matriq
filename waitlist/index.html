Questions

Asked honestly, answered straight.

Is it really free?

The Vault, tools and core AI are free. Magic Plus — deeper AI features like the study agent and handwriting reading — is a paid upgrade. The first 500 on this waitlist get it free for a semester.

How does the AI work with no data?

You download a small AI model once, on Wi-Fi if you can. After that it runs entirely on your phone. Ask questions, generate study maps, check your understanding — all offline. Your materials never leave your device.

Where do the past questions come from?

From students. Your seniors upload what they have; moderation keeps junk out; everyone gets access. One upload from you keeps it alive for the set behind you.

Which schools get it first?

We're opening department by department, starting with the largest waitlists. Add yours now — the count decides the order.

Android only?

For now, yes — most Nigerian students are on Android, so we're going deep there first. iOS follows once the Android experience is perfect.

I run an association. What's in it for us?

A dashboard for dues, verification, announcements and events — with transparency your members can see. Tell us your position in the form and we'll reach out about onboarding your association.
