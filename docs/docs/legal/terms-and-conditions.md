## 1. Eligibility

Matriq is intended for use by students enrolled at, or admitted to, participating institutions,
and by the executives of participating student associations. By creating an account, you
represent that the information you provide (including matric number or JAMB registration number)
is accurate and belongs to you.

## 2. Account creation and responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all
activity under your account. Notify us immediately if you believe your account has been
compromised. Registration follows one of two paths, described in full in
`docs/onboarding-flows.md`:
- **Staylite:** for students with existing university portal access.
- **Fresher:** for incoming students without portal access yet, using a JAMB registration number
  as provisional identification until a matric number is assigned.

Fresher accounts are provisional until the underlying identity is confirmed; certain features
may be limited until that confirmation occurs.

## 3. Acceptable use

You agree not to: provide false identity information; attempt to forge, replay, or falsify
payment or receipt data; attempt to access another user's account or another association's data
without authorization; use the platform to harass, defraud, or mislead other users; or attempt
to interfere with or disrupt the platform's operation or security.

## 4. Association relationships

Matriq is a facilitation platform. Student associations remain independently responsible for
their own governance, use of collected dues, and decisions communicated through the platform
(e.g., announcements, event details). Matriq is not a party to, and does not adjudicate,
disputes between a student and their association regarding how dues are used.

## 5. Payments and transaction processing

- Dues payments are processed through our third-party payment provider (Paystack). By initiating
  a payment, you authorize the charge for the displayed amount.
- Payment status shown in the app reflects verified confirmation from the payment provider — see
  `docs/payment-integration.md` for the technical guarantee behind this.
- Digital receipts are generated upon verified successful payment and include a QR code for
  in-person verification by association executives.

## 6. Refunds and payment disputes

- Refund requests are subject to the policies of the relevant association and, for gateway-level
  issues (e.g., a charge that failed but was still deducted), the payment provider's own dispute

## 7. AI Study Companion — features and limitations

- The AI Study Companion is a self-hosted study aid intended to help with course material and
  exam preparation. It is **not** a substitute for official course instruction, and its answers
  should be verified against your actual course materials and instructors.
- The AI Study Companion may occasionally provide inaccurate or incomplete information. Do not
  rely on it as the sole source for any graded or high-stakes academic decision.
- Content you submit for AI ingestion (past questions, materials) may be reviewed/moderated
  before being made available to other users, per `docs/ai-model.md`.
